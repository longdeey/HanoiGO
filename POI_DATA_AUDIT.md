# HanoiGO POI data audit

Checked on 2026-09-16. The 122-place demo catalog combines the original development seed with curated experiences covering central Hanoi, Hòa Lạc, Ba Vì, Sóc Sơn, craft villages, food, festivals, nature and accommodation. Coordinates are stored as source-backed snapshots or curated editorial coordinates. Ordinary adult prices and opening hours are **verified** only when a venue or government source is recorded in [`curated_places.json`](backend/data/curated_places.json) (`price_verified` / `hours_verified` with a `price_source` / `hours_source` link). **Estimate** means the value is still a development assumption. The itinerary UI shows field-level status for scheduled stops.

**Famous places added 2026-10-11:** 29 well-known sights and craft villages (Perfume Pagoda, Thay, Tay Phuong and Tram Gian pagodas, Va and Phu Dong temples, Ho Chi Minh Museum, the Presidential Palace relic site, Lotte observation deck, Dong Ngac and the ethnic culture village, Chuong, Quang Phu Cau and other craft villages…) and 8 recurring festival events. Coordinates and `source` come from the OpenStreetMap element; prices, fees and durations are planning estimates. Twenty existing landmarks were raised to `must_see` / `notable`. Tet, Hung Kings, Hang Ma Mid-Autumn and the new festivals recur on the lunar calendar (`annual.calendar = "lunar"`).

**Data clean-up 2026-10-12:** 29 curated rows that were invented or unverifiable were retired: twelve made-up hotels, eight Hòa Lạc/Thạch Thất venues, generic "food stop" experiences ("Trải nghiệm bún thang Cầu Gỗ", "Cà phê phố đường tàu"…), Bún chả Hàng Quạt (only a generic OSM match) and two vague holiday "programmes". Every remaining curated place cites an OpenStreetMap element or an official page; 46 coordinates were corrected against OSM, including Núi Hàm Lợn (~9 km off), Hồ Đồng Đò (~11 km) and Đền Sóc (~4.5 km). Sixteen named local eateries with OSM addresses were added (Bún chả Hương Liên, Bún chả Đắc Kim, Chả cá Lã Vọng, Phở Sướng, Bánh cuốn Thanh Vân, Miến lươn Đông Thịnh, Phở cuốn Hương Mai…) plus Cột cờ Hà Nội, Chùa Đậu, Hồ Quan Sơn and Ao Vua.

**Coverage:** the catalog currently contains 150 places, including accommodation suggestions that are shown separately from timed activities. Verified price/hour fields remain concentrated in the original source-backed records; curated event and nature records carry an information source and explicit date window where applicable. Seasonal records currently cover Hàng Mã lantern walks (11–25 September 2026), the Old Quarter Moon Night (25 September 2026), Sơn Tây main night (26 September 2026), the Đường Lâm moon night (19 September 2026), plus recurring planning windows for Tết, Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương, 30/4, Quốc khánh 2/9 and Christmas. Lunar holiday windows are intentionally approximate until each year's official calendar is confirmed. Event schedules, restaurant menus, access restrictions and ride fares can change; recheck sources before public travel advice.

## OpenStreetMap import (2026-09-16)

`backend/app/infrastructure/db/osm_import.py` adds 3,415 active places from OpenStreetMap (ODbL) on top of the 150 curated rows (rows that duplicate curated places are skipped at import); 120 of Hanoi's 126 communes and wards have at least one place; they are recognisable by the `-osm-<element id>` slug suffix and by `source` pointing at the OpenStreetMap element. Per category: 500 food, 300 coffee, 263 history, 200 parks, 160 accommodation, 150 shopping, 82 local experiences, 34 museums, 25 hiking peaks and 12 nature spots; 182 rows lie more than 12 km from Hoàn Kiếm and carry the `outskirts` tag (Ba Vì, Sơn Tây, Sóc Sơn, Đông Anh, Gia Lâm, Thạch Thất and others).

| Field | Status | Provenance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, coordinates, category | Snapshot of OpenStreetMap tags (name, name:en, name:vi, tourism, historic, amenity, leisure, natural, shop, cuisine) | [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), raw elements cached locally in `backend/data/osm_hanoi_raw.json` by `fetch` (git-ignored) |
| Opening hours | 473 rows parsed from the OSM `opening_hours` tag (`hours_source` = element URL); the rest use category defaults | Estimate unless `hours_source` is set |
| Prices, entrance fees, visit duration | Category and cuisine estimates (e.g. street food 70,000 VND, restaurant 120,000 VND, museum 40,000 VND) | Estimate; `price_verified` is false for every imported row |
| Local score | 56–84 from tag richness (Wikipedia/Wikidata, heritage, website, hours, cuisine); franchises and non-Vietnamese kitchens rank lower and never carry `local_culture` | Heuristic |

Imported rows are never marked verified and the itinerary UI shows their estimate status field by field. Re-running `fetch` refreshes the raw cache; `remap` re-applies the mapping rules offline; rows that leave the snapshot are deactivated, not deleted.
